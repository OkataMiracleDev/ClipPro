# Presets

A preset is a complete set of tuning: thresholds, lengths, weighting, sub-metrics, flag positions and camera movement. Pick one from the menu at the top of the inspector.

## Built-in presets

| Preset | For |
|---|---|
| All-Rounder | A balanced default for any footage |
| Talking Head | Interviews and dialogue: steady, sharp, well exposed |
| Social Media | Short, punchy, well-exposed moments |
| Cleanup | Keeps almost everything, removing only junk |
| Action / Sport | Movement and energy |
| Long Takes | Long, stable shots |
| Drone / Aerial | Smooth, sharp gimbal and drone footage |
| Whip Pans | Fast horizontal whips in either direction |
| Locked-Off | Tripod-steady, static shots |
| Twist | Rolling, rotating camera moves |
| Tilt | Vertical camera moves |
| Pan | Horizontal camera moves |
| Interview | Talking Head plus faces in focus and complete sentences |
| Wedding / Event | Tolerant of dim venues, wary of LED flicker |
| B-roll | Clean, steady cutaways of a usable length |
| Documentary Verité | Handheld observational footage |
| Product / Tabletop | Pin-sharp, well-exposed product shots |
| Gimbal Walk | Smooth moving gimbal shots |
| Audio-First | Podcasts and vlogs: clean, present speech |

Interview's face and sentence measures need the optional AI modules; without them, those parts have no effect.

## Your own presets

From the preset menu:

- **Save as preset…** stores the current tuning under a new name.
- **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** manage your presets (built-ins can't be changed).
- **Export** and **Import** use readable `.toml` files you can share or keep in version control.
- When your tuning differs from the chosen preset, the menu shows it as modified and lists every change, each with its own revert.

The camera presets (Pan, Tilt, Twist, Whip Pans) show the clips that match best as soon as you choose them.
