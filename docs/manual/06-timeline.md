# Timeline, chart and selects

## Timeline

Every clip sits in a row:

- **Score bar**: red to green, sample by sample.
- **Selects**: outlined blocks. Click one to play it.
- **Flags**: lanes under the score bar.
- **Audio**: the waveform, when the clip has sound.

Scroll to move along it and `Ctrl`+scroll to zoom. The expand button gives the timeline more room.

## Score chart

Each dot is a select: across is its length, up is its average score. The dashed lines are the score threshold; drag them. Hover a dot for details and click it to jump there. The current select has a ring, and the other selects of the same clip a fainter one. The header shows how many selects there are, their total length and what share of the footage that is.

## Selects only

**Selects only** (the list icon in the title bar) packs the selects together, like a rough cut, without changing anything. Turn it off to see whole clips again.

## Sorting

The sort menu orders clips and selects by timeline order, score (high to low or low to high) or length (long to short or short to long). Exports follow the same order.
