# Projects

A project is one `.clippro` file. It holds the clip list, the measurements, your tuning and your decisions; your media stays where it is.

- **Saving** is automatic: every change is stored as you make it. **Save as…** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`) makes a copy under a new name.
- **Recovery**: if ClipPro closes unexpectedly, the project opens where you left off.
- **Recent projects** are on the start screen, and ClipPro can reopen the last one at launch (Settings → General).

## Bins and smart collections

- Create a bin with the folder button beside **Bins** and drag clips onto it. A clip lives in one bin at a time; drag it to **All clips** to take it out.
- A **smart collection** is a saved search, for example "camera contains FX6 and best score at least 60". Its contents update as clips are analysed and as you tune.

## Templates

A template stores your tuning and watch folders, so a new project can start the way you usually work. Save one from the Snapshots menu (the camera button beside undo and redo) under **Project template**; new projects can start from it on the start screen.
