# Local AI

ClipPro can also look at what is *in* your footage: who is on screen, what kind of shot it is, and what people say. All of it runs on your PC with open models. Your footage never leaves it.

## Turning it on

Open **Settings → AI Models** and choose **Turn on local AI**. Then pick the modules you want:

| Module | What it adds | Download |
|---|---|---|
| Faces and People | Finds faces, groups them into people, scores faces in focus, the **No face** flag | about 39 MB |
| Shot types and visual search | Wide, medium, close-up and more; an aesthetic score; search by describing a shot | about 155 MB |
| Transcripts | Searchable transcripts, the **Flub** flag, sentence snapping, subtitles | 78 MB to 1.6 GB, depending on the model |
| Speakers | Who speaks when | about 25 MB |

Choose **Download models**. Each file is checked against its published checksum, and an interrupted download resumes where it stopped. The installed list shows each model's licence and size, and you can delete any of them.

For transcripts, pick a model size: **Small** is the best balance for most PCs, and **Large v3 Turbo** is the most accurate. Set the language when you know it; otherwise it is detected per clip.

**Deterministic AI** runs everything on the CPU, so the same footage always gives the same results. It is slower; leave it off unless you need repeatable results.

## When it runs

AI analysis starts by itself once the core analysis has finished, and the status bar shows its progress. It always yields to core analysis: if you import more footage, the AI pauses until those clips are analysed. Use the **×** in the status bar to stop it, or **Analyse now** in Settings to start it straight away.

## People

The **People** list in the sidebar shows everyone found across the project, with how many clips they appear in. Click a person to see their clips.

- **Rename**: double-click a name, or right-click and choose **Rename…**.
- **Merge**: if one person was split in two, right-click one and choose **Same person as ›**.

Names are kept when new footage is added and the people are regrouped.

## Transcripts and search

Open the **Transcript** tab in the right panel. It shows the selected clip's transcript, line by line; click a line to jump there.

- Filler words ("um", "uh") are struck through, and words the model was unsure of are underlined with dots.
- Lines with a restart or repeated phrase are marked **flub**.
- In interviews with more than one voice, lines are labelled S1, S2, and so on.
- **SRT** and **VTT** save the transcript as subtitles.

The search box above it searches the whole project in two ways:

- **Spoken words** finds what people say.
- **What's on screen** finds shots from a description, such as "boat at sunset" or "close-up of hands".

## Scoring with AI

With AI on, the **Content** group appears in Weighting:

- **Face in focus** and **Face size** favour sharp, well-framed faces.
- **Aesthetic** favours pleasing, well-lit frames.
- **Shot-type match** favours the shot types you pick with the chips under it.

The **Interview** preset uses these. Two more switches appear under **Selects**:

- **Snap to sentences** moves select edges to the nearest sentence start or end (within 1.5 s) so cuts don't land mid-word.
- **Best take only** keeps just the best of several takes of the same shot or line. The select editor shows "Take 2 of 3" and lets you jump between takes.

Smart collections can also match **Person**, **Shot type**, **Speakers** and **Setting** (indoor, outdoor, day or night). Stringouts can be grouped by **Person**, **Shot type** or **Take group**, with the best take first in each group. Markers can include `{transcript}`, `{people}`, `{shot_type}` and `{take}` (see Export).
