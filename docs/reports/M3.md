# Milestone M3: Parity

Date: 2026-10-30 · Branch `dev`

**Result:** ClipPro now does what Cutdown does for the core workflow:
1. Import cards or folders and analyse them.
2. Tune with the same controls: thresholds, weighting radar, sub-metric sliders, flag tiers, the camera wheel, and presets.
3. Review clips in a viewer with overlays and an inspector that explains every decision.
4. Export to Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, Avid (ALE and markers), OTIO, EDL and reports.

There are 19 built-in presets, against Cutdown's 12. The extras are custom presets, watch folders, overrides and ratings, bins and smart collections, undo/redo with snapshots, and log previews.

## Beyond Cutdown (built so far)
- **Tuning:** undo/redo (500 steps), snapshots and A/B compare.
- **Selects:** manual overrides, star ratings, colour labels and notes.
- **Organising:** bins and smart collections; recent projects and templates.
- **Viewer:**
  - SharedBuffer frame transport with decode-ahead, and an audio master clock.
  - Before/after split, and A/B compare of two selects.
  - Log profiles guessed from camera metadata; score-time Luma uses the same transforms.
- **Exports:** HTML report and PDF contact sheet.
- **CLI:** `analyze`, `score`, `export`, `info`, `presets`.
