# ClipPro 1.0.0

The first public release. ClipPro scores every second of your footage for focus, exposure, motion and sound, and hands Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid a timeline of only the good parts.

## Analysis and selects

- Focus, exposure, camera and subject motion, and audio measured on every sample, with flags for blur, shake, clipping, low light, wind, hum and more
- Presets for interviews, b-roll, drone, events and more, tunable with sliders, a weighting radar and a camera-movement wheel
- Frame-accurate viewer with log previews (S-Log3, LogC, V-Log and others), overlays and a frame inspector

## Your edit

- Exports to FCP7 XML, FCPXML, AAF (beta), OTIO, EDL and ALE, with scored markers
- Review an existing edit, see every shot's score and swap in better takes
- Multicam sync, stringouts, proxies, rendered selects and verified card offload

## Local AI (optional, on your PC)

- People from faces, shot types, visual search and transcripts with filler and flub detection
- Snap selects to sentences and keep only the best take

## Updates

- ClipPro checks for new versions at start-up and shows what's new; nothing installs until you choose to
